### What is DevOps?
DevOps is a practice adopted by companies to make the process of delivering software 
quick and efficient to the end users.

To achieve this, devOps engineers ensures:
1. There is **automation**. Automation saves us from manual work -> reducing mistakes and potentially speeding up the delivery process.
2. **Quality**. Just by automating things, we cannot ensure that quality is maintained. 
3. **Monitoring**. Even after automating the software delivery process and ensuring that quality is intact, issues (bugs, broken features) may arise. So, there should be someone monitoring the process. 
4. **Testing** of the final product is done before we ship to end users.
